### 🗺️ Real-Time Strategic Overlay
AetherScout projects a translucent, customizable grid directly onto your gaming display, highlighting environmental choke points, elevation changes, and high-value loot zones. It's like having a cartographer in your corner, constantly redrawing the map to reflect the current state of the game.

### 📊 Post-Match Battle Analysis
After each session, AetherScout generates a comprehensive **tactical dossier**, breaking down your movement patterns, engagement distances, and survival time. This isn't just a scoreboard—it's a masterclass in self-improvement, offering concrete suggestions for flanking routes and positioning.

### 🌐 Multilingual Commander Interface
Speak your language. The interface supports **twelve major languages** including English, Spanish, German, French, Russian, Korean, Japanese, and Chinese. No matter where you are in the world, AetherScout speaks your tactical dialect.

### 📱 Responsive Companion Dashboard
Whether you're on a 4K ultra-wide monitor or a compact laptop screen, AetherScout's dashboard adapts fluidly. The **mobile-optimized companion app** allows you to review your last match's heatmaps from your phone during a lobby break.

### 🔒 Privacy-First Architecture
Your gameplay data remains on your device. AetherScout operates on a **local-first model**, ensuring that your tactical information never leaves your system without your explicit consent.
